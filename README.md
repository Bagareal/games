# Free For Real

*Free games. For real.*

A collection of free, browser-based games. No ads, no payment requirements — just open a link and play.

## Philosophy

Everything here is free to play, forever. There are no in-app purchases that unlock content behind a paywall, no ad networks, and no tracking. An account is entirely optional: play as a guest with progress saved only in your browser, or create an account (just a nickname + password — no email, no personal data) to sync your progress across devices and appear on each game's leaderboard. If a game has an in-game currency or "shop", it's just a gameplay mechanic — never a real transaction.

**About accounts**: since no email is attached to your account, if you lose both your password *and* your recovery code, there is no way to recover your account or its progress — save your recovery code somewhere safe when you create an account.

## Games

### [Wordio](wordio/)

A word-finding puzzle: spell out all the target words using the letters on a wheel, in Italian, English, or French. Play it at:

**[freeforreal.app/wordio](https://freeforreal.app/wordio/)**

### [Boing](boing/)

A brick-breaker: bounce the ball off your paddle to clear every brick. 3 lives, falling power-ups (wider paddle, extra life, multi-ball, slower ball), 3 difficulty levels, and levels that get tougher as you go. Small, deliberately rough pixel-art look. Play it at:

**[freeforreal.app/boing](https://freeforreal.app/boing/)**

### Blokko *(coming soon)*

A falling-blocks puzzle game — clear lines before they stack up.
