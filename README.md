# Free For Real

*Free games. For real.*

A collection of free, browser-based games. No ads, no accounts, no payment requirements — just open a link and play.

## Philosophy

Everything here is free to play, forever. There are no in-app purchases that unlock content behind a paywall, no ad networks, and no tracking beyond what's strictly needed to save your own progress locally in your browser (nothing is sent to a server). If a game has an in-game currency or "shop", it's just a gameplay mechanic — never a real transaction.
